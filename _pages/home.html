Socially Intelligent Autonomy

Human–Autonomy Interaction · AI-Enabled Reasoning & Learning

We investigate how humans and autonomous systems interact, respond to, and adapt to one another. Our research integrates human behavior and cognitive modeling with AI-enabled reasoning, learning, and decision-making to advance socially intelligent, human-centered autonomous systems.

Intelligent Mobility & Transportation Systems

Connected & Autonomous Vehicles · Transportation Safety · Traffic Operations

Our research also advances intelligent mobility and transportation systems, including connected and autonomous vehicles, human driving behavior, transportation safety, and traffic operations. We investigate how intelligent transportation technologies influence the safety, efficiency, and performance of transportation systems.
