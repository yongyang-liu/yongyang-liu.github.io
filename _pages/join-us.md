## Ph.D. Opportunities

The Socially Intelligent Autonomy, Reasoning, and Learning Laboratory (STARLab) at Tennessee Technological University welcomes highly motivated Ph.D. students interested in advancing artificial intelligence, robotics, and autonomous systems.

**We are recruiting prospective Ph.D. students with anticipated start dates in Spring/Fall 2027.**

Our research focuses on developing human-centered, interpretable, and adaptive autonomous systems that can perceive, reason, learn, and interact effectively with humans in complex real-world environments.

Students will have opportunities to pursue interdisciplinary research in areas including:

- Human-centered and socially intelligent autonomy
- Learning-based decision-making and control
- Generative AI for autonomous systems
- Human behavior, cognition, and human-robot interaction
- Simulation and human-in-the-loop experimentation

## Desired Background

We welcome applicants with:

- Academic backgrounds in Mechanical Engineering, Electrical and Computer Engineering, Computer Science, Transportation Engineering, or related disciplines.
- Strong interests in artificial intelligence, robotics, autonomous systems, and human-centered autonomy.
- Programming experience in Python, C++, or related languages.
- Knowledge of or interest in machine learning, robotics, control systems, and computational modeling.
- Interest or experience in simulation, experimental research, human-in-the-loop studies, or real-world validation.
- Strong motivation for independent research, interdisciplinary collaboration, and scientific innovation.

Applicants do not need to have prior experience in every research area listed above.
